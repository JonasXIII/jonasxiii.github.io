Dominion Kingdom Generator

Generate random kingdom setups with advanced filtering options

What is this?

Dominion is a card-based deck-building board game. Everyone starts with the same small deck of basic cards, then spends the game buying more powerful cards out of a shared market to build an engine and rack up Victory Points.

That shared market is called the Kingdom: 10 card types chosen from the hundreds available across Dominion's many expansions. Sorting through a big box of cards to pick 10 by hand, every time, gets old fast — this tool does it for you.

Straight randomness can produce a dud Kingdom though (no way to draw extra cards, an attack with nothing to defend against it, and so on), so the settings below nudge the draw toward Kingdoms that actually play well, and the generator adds a few expansion-specific extras (Events, Projects, Shelters, and the like) automatically when the drawn cards call for them.

What the settings do

Require +Actions
Guarantees a card that grants extra actions, so you can chain multiple action cards in a turn.
Require Draw
Guarantees a card that draws extra cards.
Require Attack
Guarantees at least one card that hinders your opponents.
Require Reaction (if Attack)
If an attack card is in the Kingdom, also guarantees a Reaction card to defend against it.
Apply Blacklist
Leaves out any cards you've chosen to exclude — ones you don't own or just don't enjoy.
Base Set Only
Restricts the Kingdom to only the base Dominion set.
Two Expansions Only
Limits the 10 Kingdom cards to just two expansions, handy if you don't want to haul every box to game night.
