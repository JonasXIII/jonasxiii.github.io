Dominion Kingdom Generator

Generate random kingdom setups with advanced filtering options

What is this?

Dominion is a card based deck building board game. Everyone starts with the same small, boring deck, then spends the game buying better cards out of a shared pile to build an engine and rack up Victory Points.

That shared pile is the Kingdom: 10 card types picked from the hundreds spread across Dominion's expansions. Digging through a big box of cards to pick 10 by hand, every single time you want to play, gets old fast... But that's what this tool is for!

Pure randomness can hand you a dud Kingdom, though: no way to draw extra cards, an attack with nothing to defend against it, that sort of thing. The settings below steer the draw toward Kingdoms that are actually fun to play, and the generator throws in a few expansion specific extras (Events, Projects, Shelters, and so on) automatically whenever the cards it picked call for them.

What the settings do

Require +Actions
Makes sure there's a card that hands out extra actions, so you can chain a few action cards together in one turn.
Require Draw
Makes sure there's a card that draws you extra cards.
Require Attack
Makes sure there's at least one card that messes with your opponents.
Require Reaction (if Attack)
If there's an attack card in the mix, this makes sure there's a Reaction card too, so someone's got a way to defend against it.
Apply Blacklist
Skips any cards you've told it to leave out — ones you don't own, or just don't enjoy playing with.
Base Set Only
Sticks to just the base Dominion set.
Two Expansions Only
Keeps the 10 cards to just two expansions — handy if you don't want to lug every box to game night.
